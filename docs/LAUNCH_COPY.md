## 3. Sleep consultants

Pediatric sleep consultants charge families hundreds of dollars for a sleep plan, so a free
unlock for their clients is a small, useful extra, not competition. Aim for about 20.

### Finding them (about an hour)

1. **Graduate directories:** the Institute of Pediatric Sleep and Parenting
   (instituteofpediatricsleep.com, graduates list) and the Family Sleep Institute
   (familysleepinstitute.com, find a consultant).
2. **Search:** "pediatric sleep consultant" + "graduated" or "check-ins" or "Ferber".
3. **Instagram:** search #sleepconsultant and #babysleepconsultant; look at bios that
   mention check-ins.
4. **Pick the right ones:** consultants whose website mentions check-ins, graduated
   extinction or the Ferber method. Skip those who are strictly "no-cry" or "gentle only":
   the app won't fit their method, and it would feel pushy.

Keep a simple list: name, website, email, what their site says about check-ins, date
emailed, reply.

### The free codes

In App Store Connect, create an **offer code** for the `checkin_unlock` in-app purchase:
a custom code such as `SLEEPCOACH` with a limit of, say, 500 redemptions. Or one custom
code per consultant (for example `DANA-SLEEP`) so you can see who's sharing it.
Create codes **at least 3 days before** you send them: developers have reported free
codes for one-time purchases taking 48 to 72 hours to start working.

### The email

Send from **askcheckin@gmail.com** (the address on the website). One draft per consultant,
with a **personal opener** taken from their own site, and **one offer code per consultant**
(for example `ROCKABYE`) so you can see who sends clients. Sign as **Levi**. The list of 21
lives in your Gmail ("Check In launch: sleep consultant outreach list"), not in this repo.

**Subject:** `A free Ferber timer for your clients`

## Free unlocks

### Beta testers (TestFlight): nothing to set up

Purchases inside a TestFlight build are Apple test purchases: the tester taps
**Unlock for $2.99**, confirms, and is **never charged**. So friends in the beta can unlock
everything for free with no codes.

- Invite them as **external testers** (App Store Connect → TestFlight). You can send each
  an email invite, or make one public link to text around. Up to 10,000 testers.
- Apple briefly reviews the first beta build before outside testers can install it
  (usually about a day).
- Testers install Apple's **TestFlight** app, then Check In from it.
- Their unlock is stored on our server, so when they later switch to the App Store
  version on the same phone, they **stay unlocked**: a thank-you for testing. (If you'd
  rather not, the webhook can ignore test purchases; say so before the beta.)

### After launch: free codes

For sleep consultants' clients, press, giveaways and friends who missed the beta:

1. App Store Connect → your app → **In-App Purchases** → `checkin_unlock` → **Offer Codes**.
2. Choose either:
   - **one custom code** that many people can use, like `SLEEPCOACH`, with a limit on how
     many times it can be redeemed, or
   - **a batch of single-use codes**, one per person, so a code can't be passed around.
3. Make them **free**, and create them **at least 3 days** before you hand them out.
   Developers have reported free codes for one-time purchases taking 48 to 72 hours to
   start working.

**How someone uses a code:** open Check In → **Unlock** → **Have a code?** → enter it. Or in
the App Store app: tap their profile picture → **Redeem Gift Card or Code**. Either way
the app unlocks, and so does their partner's phone if they share.
