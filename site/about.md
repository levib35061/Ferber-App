# About these guides

Updated November 24, 2026

ferbertimer.com is a free guide to the Ferber method of baby sleep training, with a printable chart and a free web timer. Here's who writes it, where the information comes from, and how to tell us when something's wrong.

## Who writes them

The guides are written by the parent who makes [Check In](https://ferbertimer.com/#app), an iPhone timer for the Ferber method. We used the method with our own baby, kept losing track of the waits at 2am, and built the app and this site from what we learned. We're parents, not doctors or certified sleep consultants, and the guides don't pretend otherwise.

We use AI writing tools to help draft and edit. Every factual claim is checked against the sources listed at the bottom of each guide, and the waits in our chart are checked automatically against the same chart the app uses.

## Where the information comes from

- **Dr. Richard Ferber's own book**, *Solve Your Child's Sleep Problems* (revised edition, 2006): the method, the waits and the check-ins.
- **The American Academy of Pediatrics** (HealthyChildren.org): safe sleep, fevers and when to call your pediatrician.
- **Published studies** on behavioral sleep training, such as Mindell et al. (*Sleep*, 2006), Price et al. (*Pediatrics*, 2012) and Gradisar et al. (*Pediatrics*, 2016). When a source is a survey or a news report of a study rather than the study itself, the guide says so.

Each guide lists its sources at the end and shows the date it was last updated.

## What the guides are not

- **Not medical advice.** Sleep training is meant for healthy babies about 6 months and older. Talk to your pediatrician before you start, and stop and call them if anything seems wrong.
- **Not endorsed by Dr. Ferber.** We're not affiliated with him or his publisher.
- **Not paid for.** No ads, no affiliate links and no sponsored placements. The one thing we sell is our own app, and every page that mentions it says that we make it. [Our comparison of timer apps](https://ferbertimer.com/ferber-timer-apps-compared.html) uses only what each app's App Store page says.
- **Not tracking you.** The site has no analytics or tracking scripts. The web timer keeps its state only in your own browser. See the [privacy policy](https://ferbertimer.com/privacy.html).
