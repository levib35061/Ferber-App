Alerts

I didn't get a check-in alert. Why?

The alert needs notification permission. Open the app, go to Settings → Night mode & alerts, and make sure notifications are allowed. Then tap Send a test alert in 5 seconds and lock your phone to check it arrives. If you use a Sleep or Do Not Disturb Focus at night, see the next question.

Do alerts come through in Sleep Focus or Do Not Disturb?

Yes, if you allow them. Check-in alerts are sent as time-sensitive notifications. In iPhone Settings → Notifications → Check In, turn on Time Sensitive Notifications. Then in Settings → Focus, open your Sleep Focus and allow time-sensitive notifications.

The alert went to my Apple Watch and my phone stayed dark. Is that right?

Yes, that's how iPhone works. When you're wearing an unlocked Apple Watch and your phone is locked, alerts go to your wrist and the phone stays dark and silent. That's handy in a nursery: you get a quiet tap instead of a sound. If the watch is off your wrist or charging, the alert shows and sounds on the phone instead.

Will the alert wake the baby?

It's a short, soft sound, not an alarm. If your phone's silent switch is on, the phone only vibrates. In the app, the three firm pulses mean a check-in is due.

Does the timer keep going if I lock my phone or close the app?

Yes. The alert is scheduled with iOS the moment the wait starts, so it arrives even if the app is closed. When you open the app again, the countdown is worked out from the saved times, so it's always right.

What’s the card on my lock screen?

While a night is in progress, Check In shows a Live Activity on the lock screen (and in the Dynamic Island): the countdown to the next check-in, or how long you’ve been in the room, with two buttons such as Going in and Asleep. Tapping a button unlocks with Face ID, opens the app and logs that step. The card goes away when your baby is asleep or you tap Up for the day. If you don’t see it, check Settings → Check In → Live Activities on your iPhone. The check-in alert still arrives either way.

With two parents, who gets the alert?

Whoever is on duty. Tap I'm on on the Night screen (or Take over / Hand off) so only one phone gets tonight's check-in alerts. The parent who taps Down is put on duty automatically.

Using the timer

Why doesn't the timer start when I tap Down?

Because many babies settle without crying. The wait only starts when you tap Crying, and it pauses if you tap Stopped crying. That way the wait counts crying, not clock time, and your crying totals stay honest.

I tapped the wrong button. Can I fix it?

Yes. For 10 seconds after any tap, an Undo button shows at the top of the Night screen. Later, open History, tap the night, and tap any entry to change its time or delete it. You can also add a tap you forgot.

What does "Wait a little longer" do?

When a check-in is due, you can skip it. The next, longer wait starts right away, and it isn't counted as a check-in. Some families find fewer check-ins work better for their baby.

I forgot to log a night. Can I add it?

Yes. In History, tap + Missed night, pick the night, and enter bedtime, about how long your baby cried and how many check-ins, how many times they woke (with the crying and check-ins for those wakings together), and when they got up. Your best guess is fine. It counts like any other night in the recap and charts, and shows as totals, without made-up times. You can add any night newer than your latest logged one, up to last night. To change one, delete it in History and add it again.

How do night feeds work?

Tap Night feed. The timer pauses and feeding time isn't counted as crying. When you tap Done feeding, the waits start again from the first one if your baby cries.

What happens at a night waking?

Tap Woke up. The waits start again from the first wait for that night, the same as at bedtime.

Can I change the wait times?

Yes. In Settings → Sleep plan you can pick the standard chart, a gentler one, or edit any wait yourself. On a rough night you can also repeat last night's waits instead of moving up.

How do I end the night?

In the morning, tap Up for the day. That ends the night and shows the morning recap, so a baby who sleeps in past 7am isn’t cut short. If you forget, the night ends on its own at 11am.

Partner sharing

How do I share with my partner?

On your phone, go to Settings → Partner sharing and tap Invite my partner. Send them the 8-character code. On their phone, they open the same page, type the code, and tap Join. Both phones then show every tap within seconds.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works on one phone with no account at all. Sign in with Apple is optional: it keeps your sharing if you reinstall the app or change phones. We don't ask Apple for your name or email.

My partner's taps aren't showing up.

Both phones need a signal or Wi-Fi to sync. Open Settings → Partner sharing and tap Sync now. Taps made with no signal are saved on the phone and sent as soon as it's back online.

Who can see our nights?

Only people who joined with your invite code. Share the code only with people you trust.

Paying

What does it cost?

Your first two nights are free. After that, a one-time $4.99 purchase unlocks the app for good. There's no subscription and there are no ads.

Does my partner have to pay too?

No. When one of you buys the unlock, everyone sharing with you is unlocked.

I bought it, but the app is asking again.

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchases. This works on a new phone or after reinstalling, as long as you use the same Apple ID. If your partner was the one who paid, join their family in Settings → Partner sharing instead.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Your data

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. It only leaves your phone if you turn on partner sharing, so that both phones can sync. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Help & about and tap Delete my account and data. This removes your nights from this phone and deletes your account on our server.

How do I start over with a new round, or a new baby?

Go to Settings → Sleep plan and tap Start a new training round. The night count goes back to night 1.
