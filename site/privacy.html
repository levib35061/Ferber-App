Privacy Policy

Check In: Baby Sleep Timer · Effective November 16, 2026

  • Your nights stay on your phone unless you turn on partner sharing.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
  • If you share with a partner, the shared data is stored securely so both phones can sync, and only people in your family can see it.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

This policy explains what the Check In: Baby Sleep Timer iPhone app ("Check In", "we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Check In is made by an independent developer.

What the app stores

On your phone

Everything you enter is saved on your iPhone so the app works offline:

  • Your baby's name and birth date (both optional)
  • Your sleep log: the times you tapped Down, Crying, Going in, Back out, Asleep, Woke up and similar, plus any notes
  • Your interval chart and app settings

If you never turn on partner sharing, this information never leaves your phone (apart from your phone's own backups, such as iCloud backup, which Apple controls).

If you turn on partner sharing

To let two phones see the same nights, the app sends this to our database so your partner's phone can download it:

  • The baby details, sleep log, notes and chart listed above
  • An account identifier: a random ID created by the app. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us a unique ID for you and, if you choose to share it, an email address (which can be an Apple "Hide My Email" address). We use these only to sign you in.
  • Your family membership and invite code, and which account recorded each tap

Only signed-in members of your family can read or change your family's data. Anyone you give your invite code to can join your family and see its nights, so share it only with people you trust.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and your purchase records (such as which product you bought and when) so the app can unlock for you and the rest of your family, and so purchases can be restored.

Notifications

Check-in alerts are scheduled on your phone. If you use partner alerts, the app stores a push-notification token for your device so we can send your phone an alert when your partner logs a tap. These alerts are delivered through Apple's push notification service (and Expo's push service, which passes them to Apple).

Videos on our website

Some pages on ferbertimer.com show our YouTube videos. Nothing loads from YouTube until you tap play. Then the video plays from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that video.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks in the app
  • No tracking you across other companies' apps or websites
  • No analytics or behavior-tracking tools in the app
  • No selling or renting your data, ever

How we use information

Only to run the app's features: the timer and alerts, your logs and charts, syncing between partners, unlocking purchases, and answering your support requests. We may also see aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics that Apple provides to developers if you've allowed sharing with app developers in your iPhone settings.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Shared data is stored on servers in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and database access rules allow each family to read and change only its own data.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone: deleting the app deletes everything stored on it.
  • Stop sharing: in Settings → Partner sharing, "Stop sharing on this phone" removes you from your family. Your partner keeps the shared nights.
  • Delete everything we store: in the app, go to Settings → Help & about → Delete my account and data. Or email us at askcheckin@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and, if you're the last person in your family, all of its shared data, within 30 days. If your partner is still in the family, the shared nights stay with them unless they ask for deletion too.

Shared data is kept while your family is using it and deleted on request as above.

Children

Check In is for parents and caregivers and isn't directed to children. The only information about a child is what a parent chooses to enter (a name, birth date and sleep log). The parent controls it and can delete it at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to how it's used. To make any request, email askcheckin@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, and it loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
